# Changelog

All updates and patches for **AIDS-Project** are documented here.

## [0.0.2] - October 23, 2026
### Improvements & Architecture
- **Narrative Engine Stability:** Refactored the internal narrator personas framework to ensure robust performance, clean type safety, and seamless integration with our local AI runtime (`dolphin-qwen2.5:3b`).

### Added
- **18+ Age Verification & Content Warning:** Added a mandatory age verification screen upon launching the game, featuring clear terms and intuitive options for adult players before entering the main menu.

### Initial Release
- **Project Foundation:** Initialized the core architecture for the AI-Driven Live 2.5D Visual Novel on Ren'Py 8.3.4.
- **Unrestricted AI Integration:** Set up support for local offline execution with zero censorship or safety filters.
- **Narrator Personas:** Introduced the initial four narrative director personas (Optimistic, Melancholic, Fatalistic, and Chaotic) to dynamically guide story tone and endings.
